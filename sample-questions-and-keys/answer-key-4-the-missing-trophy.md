# Answer Key — The Missing Trophy

Notes for grading: Q5 and Q7 are opinion/inference-based with no single correct answer — grade on whether the reasoning is supported by the text, not on which position the student takes.

1. **Literal.** The chess trophy was missing, replaced by a folded note.

2. **Inferential.** Daniel was the student assigned to lock up the library on Fridays that term.

3. **Literal.** He noticed the case seal was loose and worried it would actually be stolen; he also wanted people to finally fix the case after his earlier note to the office was ignored.

4. **Vocabulary.** "Baffled."

5. **Opinion/inferential.** Open-ended. Accept any well-reasoned answer, whether the student agrees or disagrees, as long as it references the story (e.g. it got attention and fixed the problem, but it also worried people unnecessarily / could have been reported more directly).

6. **Inferential.** Priya noticed the handwriting was "neat, almost too neat, like someone had taken their time."

7. **Inferential/opinion.** Accept reasonable answers such as: the note may have been overlooked, not taken seriously, or lost among other tasks — the passage doesn't state the exact reason, so judge based on reasonable inference.

8. **Summary.** Daniel, worried about the loose case seal, moved the trophy and left a note to draw attention to the problem; the trophy was returned once the case was properly fixed with a new lock.
