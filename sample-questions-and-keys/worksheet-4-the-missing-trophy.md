# Reading Comprehension — Year 6
## The Missing Trophy

Read the passage carefully, then answer the questions in full sentences.

---

The school's chess trophy had stood in the library display case every year since 1998. On Monday morning, it was gone. In its place was a small folded note that read: *Ask the person who locks up last on Fridays.*

Mr Whetu, the librarian, was baffled. The case had been locked, and there was no sign it had been forced open. He called the office, and soon a small crowd of students had gathered outside the library, whispering theories. Some said it was a prank. Others insisted it had been stolen for real.

Priya, captain of the chess club, wasn't convinced by either idea. She studied the note carefully. The handwriting was neat, almost too neat, like someone had taken their time. She thought about who locked up the library on Fridays — that was always a job given to a senior student as a reward for good behaviour, and this term, it had been assigned to Daniel, a quiet boy who had joined chess club only a few weeks earlier.

Priya found Daniel at lunch, sitting alone with a book. "Did you take the trophy?" she asked, straight out.

Daniel looked startled, then sighed. "I didn't steal it," he said. "I moved it. The case seal was loose — I noticed it last Friday, and I was worried someone actually would take it if I didn't say something. I thought if it just disappeared, people would finally fix the case properly instead of ignoring my note to the office last month."

Priya folded her arms. "You could have just told a teacher."

"I did," Daniel said quietly. "Nobody read it."

By the end of the day, the trophy was back in its case — with a brand-new lock, fitted the very same afternoon.

---

### Questions

1. What was different about the display case on Monday morning?

2. What clue made Priya suspect Daniel rather than someone else?

3. Why did Daniel decide to move the trophy instead of just reporting the loose seal again?

4. Find a word in the passage that means "confused, unable to understand."

5. Do you think Daniel's plan was a good way to solve the problem? Explain your answer using ideas from the passage.

6. What evidence in the story suggests the note was written carefully, not in a rush?

7. Why do you think nobody had fixed the case seal earlier, based on what Daniel says?

8. In your own words, summarise what actually happened to the trophy.
